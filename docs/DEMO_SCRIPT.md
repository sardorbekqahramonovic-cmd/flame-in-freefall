# Demo video script

Space Apps asks for a short project demo video. Check the current Project Submission Guide for the length limit; the main script below runs 30 seconds, and an extended 2-minute version follows for the local pitch. Record at 1920×1080 in the dark theme with the mission set to **Lunar surface habitat**. The narration can be in Uzbek if you add English subtitles.

## 30-second version

| Time | Screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Overview hero: the yellow 1 g flame next to the blue microgravity flame. | "Fire in space doesn't rise. It hides, it spreads in strange ways, and NASA has fifty years of data on it." |
| 0:05–0:11 | Flammability Map, cursor on the cluster at 21% / 1 atm, then on the Moon-base diamond. | "EMBER puts all of it on one map. Most tests were in sea-level air. Moon bases will run at thirty-four percent oxygen." |
| 0:11–0:19 | Fire Risk Explorer: pick Nomex, click the rover preset, the verdict turns orange "Flammable". | "Pick a material and a mission. Nomex, safe on the ISS, can burn in a lunar rover, and EMBER shows the flight data behind that." |
| 0:19–0:26 | Ask EMBER: click "Why don't Earth extinguisher standards apply in microgravity?", answer with citation chips. | "Ask anything. Answers come only from NASA evidence, with citations and gap warnings." |
| 0:26–0:30 | Mission Ranker sliding a weight, then the logo. | "EMBER: NASA's fire science, ranked for the Moon and Mars." |

## 2-minute version (local pitch)

1. **Problem (0:00–0:20).** Apollo 1, Apollo 13 and the Mir fire on the timeline. "Fire is one of the deadliest risks in spaceflight. NASA has flown dozens of combustion experiments, but the results are scattered across reports and are hard to compare. Artemis habitats will use atmospheres no crew has lived in."
2. **Atlas (0:20–0:40).** Search "Nomex", open BASS, show conditions, findings and the three implication boxes, then compare BASS with Saffire I–III. "Nineteen investigations, each with its fuels, oxygen, pressure, flow, sensors and findings, linked to the NASA source."
3. **Map (0:40–1:00).** "Here is the insight a table can't give you: almost every test sits at 21% oxygen and one atmosphere. The exploration atmosphere, 34% at 8.2 psia, is covered only by Saffire IV–VI and SoFIE."
4. **Risk Explorer (1:00–1:25).** Nomex at ISS air shows "Self-extinguishing"; switch to the rover preset and it shows "Flammable". Point at the Moon marker on the curve and the gravity table. "Lunar gravity creates gentle buoyant flow, close to the speed where materials burn most easily, so the Moon could be a worst case. EMBER flags this clearly as an extrapolation, because no partial-gravity data exists yet. That is why FM2 matters."
5. **Ask EMBER (1:25–1:45).** Ask "Is Nomex flammable at 34% oxygen and 8.2 psi on the Moon?" Show the model numbers, citations, the confidence badge and the gap warnings.
6. **Impact (1:45–2:00).** Ranker and Gaps view. "Researchers find answers in minutes. Engineers get concrete actions: correct Earth tests, re-screen fabrics, size extinguishers, detect sub-micron smoke. And it runs offline, in any browser."

## Recording tips

- Use the browser at 110% zoom so the text reads on a phone.
- Hide the bookmarks bar and use a clean browser profile.
- Record the screen with OBS, add narration and English subtitles in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve.
- Keep the cursor still while you talk; move it only to point.
