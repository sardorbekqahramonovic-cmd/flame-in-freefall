Exploration Microgravity Burn Evidence Reasoner

Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, “Flame in Freefall”. Data curated from public NASA sources: the PSI Data Repository, NASA Glenn Combustion Science, NASA investigation fact sheets and A Researcher's Guide to ISS Combustion Science. Each record links to its source.

The Fire Risk Explorer is a transparent, physics-informed screening model for exploring trends. It is not a substitute for NASA-STD-6001 material certification.
